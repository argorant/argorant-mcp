description: Size a market and build a lead list with Argorant from a plain description of who you sell to
argument-hint: "[who you sell to, e.g. heads of procurement at manufacturers in Germany]"
---

Use the build-target-list skill. The request is $ARGUMENTS

If the request is empty, ask in one sentence who the user sells to (job, industry, place).

Count the matching people with Argorant, show a preview of 5 to 10 people with names hidden, and offer to save the whole search as a list. All of this is free. Do not reveal contact details or spend credits in this command. If the user wants contacts next, say what it costs and wait for a clear yes.
